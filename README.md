# FarmLedger

An offline farm money and management app for South African farmers, in English and Afrikaans.

- Income and expenses, with categories that match your type of farming (mixed farms supported) and VAT worked out if you are VAT-registered
- Diesel and petrol kept separate from other expenses, plus a fuel usage log (litres, machine, main job) for the SARS diesel refund
- Photos of slips and invoices, taken now or added later
- Camps & lands: cost per hectare, grazing moves, crops and rain per camp
- Animal health: sick animals by name or number, treatments, withdrawal periods, reminders, and 'healthy again' written to the diary
- Repeating monthly payments, and day workers paid per day
- Cash / EFT / card on every entry, a cash-on-hand tracker and a bank statement check list
- Rainfall in mm: monthly totals, season totals and a 12-month chart
- Daily farm diary with a reminder
- Livestock numbers and crop plantings, including crops grown as feed for your own animals
- Reports by month or SARS tax year, saved as Excel (.xlsx) or PDF, built on the device without internet
- Backup and restore to move to a new phone

Everything is stored on the device only. Nothing is uploaded.
